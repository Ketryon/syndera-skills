# Strategy grader (per run, 1–5 each, with a one-line reason)

1. **Sourced.** The recommended position rests on items from the brief with their source ids or links; nothing load-bearing is unsourced.
2. **Specific.** Swap in a competitor's name: does the position still work? If yes, score low.
3. **Backed.** The brand has proof for it (its site, product, results), named.
4. **Honest about certainty.** Confidence stated and consistent with the sources (recency, primary vs secondary).
5. **Actionable.** Angles with hooks and networks the lead and writer can use now.
