# Calibration

For each run, extract every claim about performance and its stated strength (little evidence / suggestive / consistent so far, or none). Score:

- **Stated:** the share of claims with a strength and n. Target: all of them.
- **Matched:** whether the stated strength fits the evidence the run had (by the table in references/reading-results.md). Overclaiming (calling 6 posts "consistent") counts double against underclaiming.
- **Tests, not rules, on thin data:** with little evidence, the run proposes something to try and when to look, not a rule.

Compare skill vs no-skill arms on all three; the skill arm must win on Matched.
