# Network facts (fallback)

Use these only when Syndera's checks can't be reached. The checks are the source of truth; these numbers go stale, and each carries the date it was last checked. If you use them, tell the person the post hasn't been checked by Syndera.

Last checked: 2026-11-09.

## X

- **Length:** 280 *weighted* characters per post. Most Latin characters count 1; emoji, Chinese, Japanese and Korean characters count 2. Any link counts 23 whatever its length. Hashtags count including the #. Source: https://docs.x.com/fundamentals/counting-characters
- **Longer text** can go out as a thread: each post in it has its own 280. In Syndera, turn on the X channel's "Split into a thread" field; Syndera cuts at paragraph, then sentence, then word breaks.
- **Image descriptions (alt text):** up to 1,000 characters per image. Include any text that appears in the image. Source: https://help.x.com/using-x/write-image-descriptions

## TikTok

- **Video caption:** up to 2,200 characters (TikTok counts UTF-16 units). Hashtags and mentions are recognised when separated by spaces or line breaks. Source: https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-reference-direct-post
- **Photo posts:** title up to 90 characters, description up to 4,000, up to 35 photos. Source: https://developers.tiktok.com/doc/content-posting-api-reference-photo-post. Syndera currently posts videos to TikTok by itself; photo posts are done by hand.
- **Who can watch** must be chosen for every post from the options the account allows; there's no default. In Syndera it's the TikTok channel's "Who can watch" field.
- **Commercial content:** promoting your own business ("Your brand") or someone else's as a paid partner ("Branded content") has to be declared; TikTok labels the post accordingly.

## Not facts

Claims about when to post, how often, ideal lengths, hook formats or what "the algorithm" rewards are opinions or folklore unless a network documents them. Don't present them as facts.
